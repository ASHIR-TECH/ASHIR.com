Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

ASHIR TECH ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website, products, and services, including the A.D.T.P (Agent Direct Transfer Protocol) application.

Information We Collect

Website Usage

Our website may collect standard anonymized analytics data (such as page views, browser type, and general location) to improve performance. We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information through passive tracking beyond what is necessary for basic site functionality.

Communications

If you contact us via email or our contact forms, we may collect your name, email address, and message content to respond to your inquiry. We only use this information for the purpose of communication.

Application Data (A.D.T.P)

A.D.T.P is designed as a peer-to-peer (P2P) direct transfer system. Transfers occur directly between your devices. We do not route your files through our servers, and we do not store, log, or have access to the content of files transferred between peers.

How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our website and services
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our services
  • Comply with legal obligations when required

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations, or legal processes
  • To protect our rights, safety, or property, or that of our users
  • With service providers who assist in operating our website (under strict confidentiality obligations)

Due to the P2P nature of A.D.T.P, no file contents are transmitted to or stored by ASHIR TECH servers.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any information we do collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to our website.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use essential cookies to maintain functionality (such as theme preferences). We do not use invasive third-party tracking cookies for advertising purposes. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.
