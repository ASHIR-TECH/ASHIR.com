About Ashir Tech

Building Africa's AI Infrastructure Layer.

Born from Ash. Rising for the World.

Ashir Tech is a pan-African technology conglomerate headquartered in Nigeria. We build software products and platforms for Africa's 1.4 billion people — not a single-product startup, but a holding company with subsidiaries each solving a specific African problem at world-class quality. ADTP is our flagship: the missing protocol for direct, encrypted, peer-to-peer file transfer between AI agents.
