Agent Direct Transfer System

A.D.T.P

ADTP is a one-on-one file transfer protocol that connects your devices directly — peer-to-peer, encrypted end-to-end, with no intermediary server. It's built for the way you actually work: your laptop to your desktop, your workstation to your homelab, one trusted machine to another. Every transfer, every agent instruction, every audit trail is between you and your peer — not routed through a third party, not stored in the cloud, not exposed to anyone else's infrastructure. A CLI for scripting, a desktop app for visual control, and an AI agent that follows your commands — all talking directly to each other.
